What this is

We opened Shapr3D on iPhone the way a new user does, followed it to your plans, and compared that page with the market.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps and publishes what it sees in State of Paywalls 2026, so we can put your paywall next to the market rather than next to an opinion. We signed up in the iPhone app, tapped through to your plans on app.shapr3d.com, read your App Store listing and your pricing page, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what you show at the moment someone decides.

Four ideas follow. Each is a test you can run, each carries a number from the report, and each is built into a clickable prototype. Benchmarks are for Productivity apps in Europe, your App Store category and your home market.

We did not redesign your app

The prototype is your blue, your mono plan tags, your square cards, your check lists and your own trial timeline. We moved things you already publish closer to the button. This is not a design pitch.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If you have already run one of these, tell us and we will go deeper on the rest.

The four ideas

Ranked by what we would test first.

Offer changes before cosmetics: in the report, plan and trial changes win more A/B tests than visual ones. Each row opens the screen in the prototype.

01

Show yearly and monthly side by side

Your iPhone app sends people to a page of four long tier cards, and only the yearly price is in them. Put Solo yearly and Solo monthly on one screen, keep yearly selected, and move Studio and Enterprise behind “Compare all plans”.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026 · apps that show two plan lengths have 67% higher conversion than apps with one.
See the screen 67% higher CRReport finding, two plan lengths vs one
02

Put your trial timeline next to the button

You already wrote it: Today full access, Day 12 a reminder, Day 14 the charge, cancel any time 24 hours before. It sits far down shapr3d.com, while the card people tap says only “Start 14-day free trial”.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026 (Europe) · 55% of trials are cancelled within 24 hours.
See the screen ARPU +12–36%Observed in winning A/B tests
03

Put your 4.7 and Editors’ Choice on the paywall

4.7 from 34K App Store ratings, Editors’ Choice and “loved by over 1 million users” are all yours. None of them appear in the plan cards, which is where someone decides whether €179.99 is worth it.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026 · only 27% of top paywalls show social proof.
See the screen CR +10–30%Observed in winning A/B tests
04

When they leave, offer monthly or the student licence

Leave the plans page today and you land back on Recents. Ask once instead: Solo monthly at €35.99 with the same 14-day trial, or your free licence for students and educators. Both are on your own pricing page.
