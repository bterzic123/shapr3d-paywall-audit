# Paywall Audit — Shapr3D

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/shapr3d-paywall-audit/

Four ranked ideas for Shapr3D's paywall, each backed by a number from Adapty's State of Paywalls 2026, plus a clickable rebuild of the paywall and the moment someone leaves it, in Shapr3D's own design. Switch **Today / Proposed** above the phone to compare.

| # | Idea | Report anchor | Outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | Show yearly and monthly side by side | 2.3: apps showing two plan lengths have 67% higher conversion | Report finding |
| 2 | Put your trial timeline next to the button | 5.3 trial explainer; Europe cancels 55% of trials within 24h (table E) | ARPU +12–36%, observed in winning A/B tests |
| 3 | Put your 4.7 and Editors' Choice on the paywall | 5.3 social proof; 3.1: 27% of top paywalls show it | CR +10–30%, observed in winning A/B tests |
| 4 | When they leave, offer monthly or the student licence | 4.5: only 4% of apps show a second paywall; it beats the main one in 45% of apps | ARPU +10–15%, based on Adapty data |

**Benchmarks:** App Store data, Oct 2025–Sep 2026, Productivity apps in Europe.

**The report:** [State of Paywalls 2026](https://adapty.io/state-of-paywalls/?utm_source=paywall-audit&utm_medium=github-pages&utm_campaign=sop_2026&utm_content=shapr3d)

**Grounding:** prices, the trial timeline, the student licence, ratings and the "1 million users" line come from Shapr3D's own App Store listing, shapr3d.com/pricing and in-app screens. Impact ranges are observed lifts from winning A/B tests in State of Paywalls 2026 and Adapty's own data, not measured results for Shapr3D.

Run locally: `python3 -m http.server 8000` in this folder, then open http://localhost:8000.
